# ShopSmart Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes ShopSmart Retail Group's sales and inventory data using Microsoft Excel 2013. The goal is to identify sales trends, evaluate product and store performance, examine returns and inventory levels, and communicate actionable findings through an executive dashboard and business report.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel 2013
- Excel Tables and formulas
- VLOOKUP for combining related data
- Data cleaning and validation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- KPI calculations
- Interactive slicers
- PDF export and reporting

## Analysis Performed

- Total revenue and average order value
- Order and customer counts
- Monthly sales trends
- Revenue by product, category, store, state, and region
- Top-performing customers and products
- Product return analysis
- Inventory valuation and low-stock identification
- Interactive executive dashboard

## Key Findings

- **Highest-revenue product:** PROD0400, with reported revenue of 21,638,851.
- **Best-performing store:** ShopSmart Lagos, with reported revenue of 1,232,193,532.
- **Highest-revenue month:** December, with reported revenue of 660,629,527.
- **Most frequently returned product:** PROD0263, with 11 recorded returns.
- **Low-stock inventory:** STR006 and STR014 each had three inventory records at or below their reorder levels.
- **Supplier analysis limitation:** The available data did not contain a product-to-supplier relationship, so the supplier supplying the most products could not be identified reliably.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate the drivers of December's sales peak and use the findings to improve seasonal sales and inventory planning.
2. Investigate the reasons for returns of PROD0263 and monitor recurring product-quality or handling issues.
3. Review low-stock products at STR006 and STR014, and improve supplier-product data mapping to strengthen procurement analysis.

## Dashboard Filters

The executive dashboard includes slicers for Year, Month, Region, Store, Category, and Membership Level.

## Limitations

Supplier-level analysis was limited by the absence of a product-to-supplier link. Revenue figures and category labels should be checked against the relevant PivotTables before final submission, particularly where store and regional totals differ.

## Deliverables

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of spreadsheet analysis to transform sales and inventory data into business findings and practical recommendations for management.
